```text
Build METAXY — an AI Business Command Centre designed for South African small, micro, and informal-to-formal business owners who run real operations through everyday messaging and need an autonomous agent they can actually see, understand, and trust.

The name METAXY comes from the Greek metaxý — "in between." It exists for the bridge between daily informal hustle and structured, formal business.

### Context & Who This Is For
Across South Africa, millions of small businesses operate on a wide spectrum. At one end are solo and informal operators—township traders, local service providers, and 9-to-5 professionals running a side business on WhatsApp after hours. At the other end are small 5-to-50 person trade teams, like a five-person plumbing crew drowning in on-site quotes, job cards, and invoices.

These owners do not fail because they lack hustle. They hit three walls:
1. Rigid automation breaks on real human life. Standard chat bots collapse the moment a customer skips greetings, shares a personal bereavement, asks for an unusual delivery date, or negotiates to pay on delivery against business policy.
2. The informal-to-formal gap feels overwhelming. Navigating company registration, tax readiness, and business banking without drowning in bureaucracy keeps growing businesses locked out of larger buyers and credit.
3. Invisible AI destroys trust. Non-technical business owners will not hand their livelihood and customer relationships to a black-box chatbot operating out of sight.

### Vision
METAXY is not a chatbot, and it is not a generic SaaS admin template. It is a living operational home where an autonomous, memory-enabled AI agent handles day-to-day customer conversations and back-office admin—while projecting every action, judgement call, and pending decision onto a calm, legible visual command centre.

The core thesis is simple: the chat thread is not the interface. When the agent reads an off-script customer message, checks availability, enforces a deposit policy, or notices the business is ready to formalise, the owner sees those realities surface as clear, actionable operational state—never buried inside a wall of messages.

### Design Philosophy
- **Visible trust over black-box magic:** Every autonomous step the agent takes—what emotional or practical context it noticed, which business rule it checked, why it paused—should feel transparent and verifiable at a glance.
- **Human authority at the edges:** The agent handles routine work autonomously, handles sensitive human moments with warmth, and pauses gracefully when a business policy is challenged so the owner makes the final call with zero friction.
- **One continuous operational memory:** Customer conversations, orders, policies, compliance readiness, local opportunities, and customer history are connected aspects of one living business—not isolated tools.
- **Grounded in South African reality:** Built around WhatsApp-first commerce, South African Rand (ZAR), practical cash-flow policies, local geographies, and real formalisation pathways (such as CIPC registration, SARS tax readiness, and VAT thresholds).

### Core Experience
When an owner opens METAXY, they should immediately feel calm control over their business. They should be able to experience a complete, living operational cycle:

1. **Off-script customer interactions handled with context and care:**
   Imagine a side-hustle baker (e.g., "Thandi's Bakes" in Soweto) whose policy requires a 50% deposit on first orders. A new customer messages on WhatsApp without a greeting, mentions their mother just passed away, asks for 40 cupcakes and a small cake for next Thursday, and asks to pay only upon delivery because money is tight.
   The owner watches in real time as the agent mirrors the WhatsApp exchange, recognises the bereavement, responds with genuine condolences, checks the baking calendar for Thursday, politely explains why it cannot grant pay-on-delivery itself, drafts the order with the payment term flagged, updates customer memory, and surfaces a clear decision prompt for the owner—allowing them to grant a one-off exception or hold the policy in a single decisive interaction.

2. **Operational admin that turns messy reality into structure:**
   Whether it is an order negotiated on WhatsApp or a field technician sending a quick site update with parts and labour hours, unstructured daily work naturally transforms into structured quotes, job tracking, and invoices without manual paperwork.

3. **The bridge from informal hustle to formal readiness:**
   As orders and trailing 12-month turnover grow, the system quietly tracks formalisation readiness and regulatory thresholds (such as when CIPC registration opens the door to a business bank account and larger corporate buyers, or where turnover sits relative to the R1M VAT threshold), surfacing clear, step-by-step guidance without legal jargon or overwhelm.

4. **Local opportunity discovery and lasting customer memory:**
   Owners can discover nearby buyers, wholesale cafés, tuck shops, or suppliers within a target distance radius and build persistent memory around every customer—allergies, preferences, payment reliability, and thoughtful follow-up timing.

### Behaviour & Interaction Model
- Keep the owner's operational workspace and the live customer communication channel connected so cause and effect are unmistakable: a message arrives, the agent's reasoning and tool checks unfold cleanly, business state updates, and owner decisions immediately flow back to the customer.
- Support switching smoothly between active operations, compliance formalisation, local opportunity discovery, and customer memory without losing context or feeling like disconnected apps.
- Include realistic, synthetic South African sample data and interactive simulation triggers so anyone opening the experience can immediately test an off-script customer scenario, observe the agent's judgement, make an exception decision, and watch the whole workspace respond.

### Visual Language & Emotional Tone
- Avoid typical "AI product" clichés: no neon-purple floating gradients, no sci-fi HUD gimmicks, no chat-box-only minimalism, and no cluttered enterprise bloat.
- Aim for precision, warmth, and operational authority—an editorial, mission-control sensibility where crisp typography, intentional spacing, subtle technical labels, and clear status signals make complex work feel effortless and calm.
- The product should feel dependable, respectful of the owner's time, and quietly empowering—like stepping into a sharp, trustworthy operations room built specifically for a growing South African business.
```
