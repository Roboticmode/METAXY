### 0:00–0:10 · Slide 1: Title
> We're [team]. METAXY is an AI agent that runs a small business's WhatsApp and admin, with a command centre where the owner sees and approves everything.

### 0:10–0:22 · Slide 2: Hook stat
> South Africa has roughly three million small businesses. By estimate, one-point-two to one-point-eight million aren't registered. Many run from one phone: WhatsApp, a notes app and the owner's memory.

*Say "roughly" and "by estimate". These figures come from the hackathon briefing, not an official count.*

### 0:22–0:40 · Slide 3: Three walls
> They don't fail for lack of hustle. They hit three walls. One: bots break the moment a client writes like a real person. Two: going formal (CIPC, SARS, POPIA) feels impossible alone. Three: nobody hands their clients to an AI they can't see.

### 0:40–0:52 · Slide 4: Meet the owners (persona introduced)
> METAXY is one sidekick from launch to run: Lerato starting an agency, Naledi freelancing through university, or a home bakery. Today: **Naledi Studio, in test week.**

### 0:52–1:02 · Slide 5: The product
> The chat is not the interface. Clients stay on WhatsApp. Naledi sees every step, both calendars and every decision, on one screen.

### 1:02–1:05 · Slide 6: Demo cue, then HAND-OFF INTO THE DEMO
Say these exact words, then switch to the app window (`Alt+Tab`):
> **"Let's watch it work."**

### 2:05–2:17 · Slide 7: Visible trust
> The agents prepare, Naledi decides. Routine work happens alone. Dates, quotes and calendar moves wait for her click. Coursework, filings and money: never.

### 2:17–2:29 · Slide 8: Compliance bridge
> That's the METAXY bridge. When the studio is ready, the agent says so, then does the legwork for CIPC, SARS and POPIA. Naledi signs. It's guidance, not legal advice.

### 2:29–2:39 · Slide 9: How it's built
> Under the hood: three agents, Client Desk, Books and Planner, on Grok, through one tool pipeline. The dashboard only renders events. No connection? A scripted engine takes over.

*If the phone panel badge said **SCRIPTED** during the demo, you may add "…which is what ran just now." If it said **GROK LIVE**, don't.*

### 2:39–2:47 · Slide 10: Outcomes
> Measured today: seven seconds from message to approval-ready draft, and no date sent without Naledi. Targets on the right.

*The ≈7 s figure is the scripted engine on a local run. With Grok live it is slower, so say "seconds, not minutes" instead.*

### 2:47–2:53 · Slide 11: Roadmap
> Next: local clients, deeper client memory, the real WhatsApp API, and voice notes.

### 2:53–3:00 · Slide 12: Team + ask
> METAXY: from hustle to formal business. Help us put it in front of real owners. Thank you.

## If you're running long (cut in this order)
1. **Slide 9** (How it's built): skip the slide and keep one sentence for Q&A. Saves 10 s.
2. **Demo step "Check collisions"**: skip it and go straight from Approve & send to Open checklist. Saves about 12 s. Mention "it also catches deadline collisions" on slide 7.
3. **Slide 11** (Roadmap): click through silently. Saves 6 s.
4. **Slide 3**: say only the three wall names. Saves about 8 s.

## If the demo breaks
Don't debug on stage. Say **"Let me show you the recording,"** play the fallback video from the same point, and keep the same lines. The fallback plan is in [`demo-run-sheet.md`](demo-run-sheet.md#fallback-plan).

## Likely questions (one-liners)
- **Is it really WhatsApp?** "A simulator today. The setup checklist for the WhatsApp Business Cloud API is in the app, and the agents sit behind an adapter, so the channel can be swapped without touching the dashboard."
- **Is it really Grok?** "With `XAI_API_KEY` set, Grok runs each step. Without it, a scripted engine runs the same pipeline offline. The badge in the phone panel shows which one ran."
- **Does anything go out without approval?** Be honest: "Dates, quotes and calendar moves always wait for Naledi. FAQ answers, the coursework refusal and scheduled reminders still send on their own today. Making every outbound message wait is our target."
- **Does it give legal advice?** "No. Every compliance task cites the official site, and the owner signs and pays."
- **Does it do students' homework?** "Never. It schedules around school. It refuses coursework, as you saw."
- **Business model?** Not decided yet. Say so honestly. Don't invent pricing on stage.
