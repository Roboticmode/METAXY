03 · Three wallsProblem

Owners don’t fail for lack of hustle. They hit three walls.

Wall 01

Rigid bots break on real life

“Can you do it by Friday?” in test week. “Just a few more logo variations.” Button flows collapse on the first human message.

Wall 02

The informal-to-formal gap

CIPC, SARS, POPIA, a business bank account. Without guidance it feels impossible, so bigger clients and credit stay out of reach.

Wall 03

Black-box AI kills trust

A non-technical owner won’t hand their livelihood and their clients to a chatbot working out of sight.

METAXY answers all three: context-aware agents · guided formalisation · visible, approvable actions03 / 12

07 · Visible trustThe agent prepares, the owner decides

Every boundary is written down, and visible.

Do alone
  • Triage messages, match the client ledger
  • Check university + studio capacity
  • Draft quotes, replies and reminders
  • Answer FAQs (rates, banking) from the brief
  • Pre-fill compliance paperwork
Ask first
  • Send a delivery date or quote to a client
  • Move calendar blocks or ask for an extension
  • Add-on quotes for scope creep
  • Complaints: handed to the owner, never guessed
Never
  • Write, edit or submit coursework
  • Sign, submit or pay a government filing
  • Send promotions without marketing opt-in (POPIA)
  • Move money: it has no payment tool
As built today · FAQ answers and scheduled reminders still send on their own · Target: every outbound message waits for approval07 / 12
