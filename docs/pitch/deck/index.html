03 · Three wallsProblem

Owners don’t fail for lack of hustle. They hit three walls.

Wall 01

Rigid bots break on real life

No greeting. A bereavement. “Can I pay when you deliver?” Button flows collapse on the first human message.

Wall 02

The informal-to-formal gap

CIPC, SARS, POPIA, a business bank account. Without guidance it feels impossible, so bigger buyers and credit stay out of reach.

Wall 03

Black-box AI kills trust

A non-technical owner won’t hand their livelihood and their customers to a chatbot working out of sight.

METAXY answers all three: context-aware agent · guided formalisation · visible, approvable actions03 / 12

06 · Live demo≈ 60 seconds

Watch for six moments.

  1. An off-script message: no greeting, a bereavement, “can I pay on delivery?”
  2. Each agent step in Agent activity: calendar, memory, policy
  3. The agent stops at the policy line and asks the owner
  4. One click: Allow once. The customer is confirmed, kindly.
  5. The agent prepares CIPC registration. The owner signs and pays.
  6. Today’s reminders go out, and a promotion without opt-in doesn’t.

07 · Visible trustThe agent prepares, the owner decides

Every boundary is written down, and visible.

Do alone
  • Read and understand messages
  • Check calendar, memory and policy
  • Draft orders; reply within policy
  • Answer FAQs from the owner’s brief
  • Pre-fill compliance paperwork
Ask first
  • Any exception to a policy, e.g. pay on delivery
  • Complaints and unknown questions: handed to the owner, never guessed
  • Owner can take over any chat; the agent pauses
Never
  • Sign, submit or pay a government filing
  • Send promotions without marketing opt-in (POPIA)
  • Move money: it has no payment tool
As shown in today’s demo · Target for the freelancer build: every outbound client message waits for approval07 / 12
