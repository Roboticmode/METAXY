# Graduate Founder Archetype — Companion Launch Use Case

> **Status:** Companion / secondary archetype (**Launch** stage).  
> **Relationship:** Precedes [student-freelancer.md](student-freelancer.md) (**Run** stage). Together they tell one story: *Launch, then Run*. METAXY works for any small business, formal or informal; the Student-Freelancer archetype is the primary slice selected for the live demo implementation.

---

## 1. Purpose

This document captures the **Launch** archetype: a graduate with technical skills who cannot find formal employment and decides to start a small tech agency. METAXY acts as their sidekick from *"where do I even start?"* through to managing their first paying client.

---

## 2. The Archetype

**Lerato Mahlangu** is a fictional 22-year-old who recently finished a BSc in Computer Science in Johannesburg. All names, figures, and prospects are synthetic.

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Situation | Graduated, 5 months of job applications, no offers |
| Skills | Builds websites and simple apps well; weak on sales, marketing, admin, and pricing |
| Goal | Start a small tech agency, solo or with one friend |
| Starting capital | Minimal; needs to earn from the first client |
| Business status | Unregistered; previously completed two unpaid jobs for friends |
| Main channel | WhatsApp for talking to prospects and clients |
| Biggest worry | *"I know how to build it. I don't know how to run a business."* |

---

## 3. The Core Problem

Starting a business requires handling four domains at once: technical delivery, sales, marketing, and legal/financial admin. Early founders are usually strong in one and stuck on the other three.

### Failure modes METAXY addresses
- **Not knowing the first step:** The process feels overwhelming, so nothing starts.
- **Compliance confusion:** Unsure what to register (CIPC), what taxes apply (SARS), or how to open a business bank account.
- **No clients:** No clear list of nearby businesses that need their service or how to pitch them.
- **Messy early operations:** Quotes, invoices, and client chats scattered across WhatsApp.
- **Isolation:** Unaware of local founder programmes, funding deadlines, or support events.

## 4. The Launch Journey (Hands Off to Run Stage)

| Stage | Question Lerato asks | What METAXY does | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Explore** | *"What can I start with my skills?"* | Asks clarifying questions; proposes 2–3 service offers and a first target market | One-page business concept |
| **2. Get compliant** | *"What do I need to register and pay?"* | Explains steps in plain language with official sources; builds a task & document checklist | Compliance checklist |
| **3. Set up** | *"How do I handle money and admin?"* | Compares business account requirements; sets up quote and invoice templates | Ready-to-use templates & simple books |
| **4. Find clients** | *"Who needs what I do?"* | Ranks synthetic local prospects by fit & distance; drafts outreach messages | Prospect shortlist + outreach drafts |
| **5. Run** | *"A client replied. Now what?"* | Hands over to `Client Desk`, `Books`, and `Planner` to manage WhatsApp, CRM, quotes, and invoices | First client managed end-to-end |

## 5. Companion Agent Roster

| Agent | Owns | Acts alone on | Asks first on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sidekick** | Onboarding conversation, launch plan, delegation | Summaries, planning, drafting | Anything sent externally |
| **Compliance Guide** | CIPC, SARS, business banking steps | Explaining requirements, building checklists | Submitting filings or recommending a binding choice |
| **Opportunity Scout** | Local prospects, funding, events | Searching, ranking, filtering closed deadlines | Contacting any prospect |
| **Client Desk** | WhatsApp replies, CRM cards, quotes | Drafts, customer notes | Sending messages or quotes |
| **Books** | Invoices and payments | Tracking due dates | Sending invoices or changing amounts |

---

## 6. Approvals, Boundaries & Advice Limits

- **Same core rule:** Agents prepare, the founder decides. Zero outbound messages, invoices, or filings occur without explicit approval.
- **Guidance, not legal/tax advice:** Every compliance step shows its public source (CIPC / SARS) and advises confirming with the relevant authority or professional.
- **Informal-friendly:** Starts with an unregistered founder and maps the path from informal hustle to formal business without assuming registration upfront.

---

## 7. Companion Synthetic Data & Scenarios

- **Local prospects (~40):** Sector, Johannesburg area, distance, website status, visible need, plus intentional poor fits to test filtering.
- **Funding & events (~12):** Fictional programmes with eligibility and deadlines, including already-closed programmes that must be filtered out.
- **Four launch scenarios:**
  1. *"I just graduated. Where do I start?"* → Concept + launch plan
  2. *"Who needs this?"* → Ranked local businesses without websites + outreach drafts
  3. *"What do I need to register?"* → Sourced CIPC/SARS checklist + obligations calendar
  4. *First client reply* → WhatsApp reply, CRM card, quote, and invoice managed end-to-end
